Who I Am

Hi, I'm Nassya Adystira

I am a final-year student pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Electronics & Telecommunications Engineering at Politeknik Caltex Riau. My academic journey is deeply rooted in my fascination with how technology connects the world.

As a proud recipient of the Riau Provincial Government's Tahfidz Full Scholarship, I continuously strive for excellence. I have developed a strong foundation and practical expertise in Internet of Things (IoT), robotics, telecommunication networks, and embedded systems. Building real-world projects that solve actual problems is what excites me the most.

Beyond technical skills, I am equipped with strong leadership, public speaking, and problem-solving abilities. I actively participate in campus organizations and international competitions, believing that collaboration and continuous learning are the keys to innovation.
